# map_paper

Rendered from `tools/materials/ptex/map_paper.ptex` (written by `tools/materials/build_ptex.py`) by Material Maker 174f15e, headless on lavapipe (`material-maker-headless` skill), at 2048 px, then `tools/materials/postprocess.py`: maps albedo, normal, orm at 1024 px, one tile 0.25 m. Status: starter. The campaign map's poster paper: aged cream, mottled, fibres, foxing; the page prints the map over it (owner, 2026-11-07: "a crusty print on old piece of paper with hexagon grid drawn on it").
