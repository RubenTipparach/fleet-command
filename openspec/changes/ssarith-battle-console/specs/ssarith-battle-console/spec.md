## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Ssarith Hunt has a battle console in its own skin

The battle console SHALL be available in the Ssarith Hunt skin, with the same rules, panels and controls as the
Concord's, the Hunt's rendered kit, its 24 icons, its type and its colour roles. Every press SHALL change the console's
state exactly as the same press does in the Concord skin.

#### Scenario: Parity
- **WHEN** the parity check presses every control of every named shot in the Ssarith page and in the Concord page
- **THEN** every press leaves the same state in both
