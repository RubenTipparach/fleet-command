From the culture

The Hunt's culture as you approved it (survey U7), each line turned into part of a ship. The readings are ours and provisional.

  • "The quarry must be worthy": they hunt alone or in pairs, so a few heavy ships, never a swarm.
  • "The stalk patient", "built to wait unseen": a dark, matte, mottled hull that holds still with its lights shuttered (Stalk).
  • "Slow and silent until they strike": claws that move only when it strikes; kill red lit only then (Strike).
  • "Big-boned reptiles with heavy clawed hands", and your note "gorn emphasizes claw like features, since they enjoy hunting": a squat broad hull, and claws as the ship's own feature.
  • "The kill earned and counted": a bone notch cut into a claw for each worthy kill, as the emblem's fang is notched.
  • "Worn gunmetal, camouflage, tracking tubes": gunmetal laid in keeled scutes under canopy and moss camouflage, green phosphor behind the windows.
  • "Squat armoured hunting ships with broad prows": the bow keeps its width to its face, a shovel, never a point.

The three directions

  • A Grasp (recommended): four hooked claws at the prow's corners, closed in front of the bow while it stalks, spread open when it strikes. The grapple projectors sit under them. A bigger class adds a pair.
  • B Jaw: the prow split into two jaws lined with fangs, a gun in the throat; the nacelles splayed low like a reptile's legs, three talons at each one's bow.
  • C Crouch: a hump of big scutes over a low prow; the nacelles folded under the belly like forelegs, a sickle claw at each.

On the bible

  • Rooms first: 34 rooms on three decks, crew 40, the middle deck widest and each room aft a little narrower, so the hull tapers aft like an animal toward its tail, and a room across the whole prow on the two lower decks, so the bow stays broad. The trophy hall, the hunters' ready room and the dens come from the culture and are placeholders.
  • The hull is the shared fitter's, then the Hunt's character goes on top of it (rule H2): no steps where a deck ends, only slopes, and a shovel prow; every room is measured 1.05 m inside.
  • Windows are the person-sized pane, one row a deck, on lived-in rooms only, lit dim phosphor green.
  • Lights keep every job the bible gives them, hooded and dimmer in Stalk so the ship still reads.
