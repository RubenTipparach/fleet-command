Fractal vessels

All Fleet Command mockups (the tracker)

Mockup 37, OpenSpec change fractal-vessels, v2. These ships have no mesh. Each pixel marches along its ray into a distance field until it reaches the surface, at half the screen's resolution unless you switch it off under Cost. Beside them is the Concord's Petrel (86 m), for scale. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom.
