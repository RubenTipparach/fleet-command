Fleet Command Music

Orchestral space opera over a synth bed (survey S1), and now music for every faction in the game, each with a battle loop and an ambient loop built from its own written culture: the Concord's theme in E flat; the Krai Warhost's Line, a ground bass that never changes with a voice added every pass (tradition kept and added to), the emperor's Crown over three houses and the tally in fives; the Ssarith Hunt's Bows, one rising figure at three sizes at once (the Bow Rites), long rests and a single strike; the Kelvane Institute's Balmer motif, hydrogen's spectral lines as pitches, each note brought to the mark and every finding repeated. 12 renders, 10.0 minutes in all.
