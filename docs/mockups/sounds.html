Fleet Command Sounds

36 sounds for the battle: every weapon mount firing, what the shots hit, the ship itself, the console keys and the alerts, and the bridge under it all. 26 are cut from real recordings (CC0 packs from OpenGameArt: a heavy laser cannon, a ship's cannon, electric charges and shield hits, real explosions, rocket motors, a computer room), slowed and layered to starship size; the console tones and three of the alerts are synthesised. The hits have short alternatives for when they come fast, and the battle stations alarm has two alternatives to choose from. Battle sounds are mono and placed in 3D at their ship or mount; the console, the alerts and the bridge bed are not. Cues marked rec are recorded.

In the game since 2026-11-10: every battle sound plays at its ship, quieter with distance, and the console's keys, alerts and bridge bed play on the console (openspec/changes/sound-effects). The weapons were redone the same day without the arcade lasers (owner: "Sfx are really goofy for the weapons"); the old and new side by side are on Drive.
