Redone on the guide

Versions 1 and 2 were drawn hull first and painted in the Krai console's own volcanic metal, red and forge orange. Version 4 follows the Ship Design Bible step by step; use Build it up to walk through it.

  • Rooms: 24 functional rooms on four decks, the middle two wide and the top and keel decks narrow, so the hull fitted round them is a blade's lozenge in section; the spinal cannon runs forward along the keel deck, so the bow is a wedge led from below.
  • Hull: fitted round them with the shared fitter, its chamfers cut as deep as the rooms allow, so the section is a blade standing on its edge.
  • Parts: a bayonet fixed under the cannon's muzzle, a dorsal crest and a keel blade (the Krai's distinct features), two blade nacelles standing on edge on swept struts, one tall forge drive. A frigate's bridge stays inside the hull (survey A18: a bridge block only on super-capitals).
  • Windows: person-sized, one row a deck, only where crew and command rooms meet the hull.
  • Exterior: blued steel with chevron plates pointing forward and tempered bronze edges, pale gold light: the forge said a second way, chosen to complement the console (rule X1, a guide; nothing measures it).
