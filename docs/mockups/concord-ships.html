Windows give the scale

Every window is person sized, 1.1 by 0.6 m, on every class, and every row is one deck, 3.4 m apart. Count them: two rows on a corvette's rim, five decks through the cruiser's saucer. Runs of 4 to 12 are rooms, gaps are ribs, about one in five is dark. The Scale view stands a 1.8 m figure beside them.

Head and legs

The disc is the head, the crown on it the brain (the bridge); the nacelles hang on bent pylons like legs. Rival empires will read as a bird's head and wings, the hunters as claws (survey A8).

Livery and markings

  • Bastion cruiser: ceremonial. Twin Signal bands, a Lilac pinstripe, the crest, few service marks.
  • Lantern frigate: working. Hatch numbers, pod rings, docking targets, walkways, red rescue marks.
  • Swift corvette: minimal. One racing stripe and the registry.

Plating tells how it was built

Cruiser: rings laid in a yard. Frigate: patchwork, plates replaced in the field. Corvette: long strakes that lead the eye forward. Each plate has its own shine.

Lit for the dark

Registry, name and crest sit in floodlight pools; lamps in the hull graze the plating; windows glow; port red, starboard green, white strobes, red beacons. Try Shadow side and No sun.
