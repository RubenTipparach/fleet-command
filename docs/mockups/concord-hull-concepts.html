From Star Crew

  • Inside out: the zones first (rooms and spaces, each with a kind, a size and decks), then a hull wrapped round them with 0.7 m clearance, then the two fitted. The Fitted stage shows the zones through the hull.
  • Windows only where a room is: they are cast from inside crew, commons, medical and command zones to the hull, one row per 3.4 m deck.
  • Three alternatives, three stages, measured: the panel's Measured block checks that every zone lies inside its hull.

From Adventure

  • One shape language per faction, from how it thinks. The Concord decides by consent and houses every kind of body.
  • The player's faction has negative space: you can see through a Concord ship, and no rival will have a hole in it.
  • A class is counted, never fattened: rings, lantern pairs or sail pairs.
  • Measured, not intended: lengths and beams come off the built geometry.

The three directions

  • A, Halo. The crew live in a ring you can see through, round a hub that holds the reactor, with the bridge raised on it at the centre of the circle. Drive pods hang outboard on short pylons.
  • B, Lantern spine. An open lattice keel strung with lit habitat lanterns, each pair its own shape, for the bodies that live in it.
  • C, Light sail. A slim keel between outboard booms, with glowing shield sails stretched between them.

Take the shape, not the text: the method is from your two projects; the ships are the Concord's own.
