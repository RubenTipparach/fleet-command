Each ship from above and from the side, as a silhouette, at 40 px and 100 px. The family reads as a faceted hull with two long nacelles held off it on level pylons.

What every ship takes from the Tern

  • An octagonal hull lofted through stations along its length, every corner chamfered, built from the same five numbers per station as Star Crew's exteriors.json.
  • White angular armour over a grey subhull, a narrow grey service band at the waterline with its pipe and clamps, and grey channels running down the back.
  • Windows on three decks 3.5 m apart, each a warm pane in a copper rim, only where crew live.
  • Long octagonal nacelles on level swept pylons, a glowing field strip down each side, a copper intake at the front.
  • Radiator banks aft on the back, turret housings, twin engine bells, painted registry on the dorsal plating and an identification stripe that wraps the hull.

What is the Concord's own

  • The livery: Concord sky blue stripes, dark seams either side (the Tern's other liveries are one tap away for comparison).
  • A raised command block on every class, glazed at the front, so the bridge is always the first thing you find.
  • A class is counted by its nacelles: the corvette and frigate carry one pair, the cruiser two (a level pair and a ventral pair).
  • Bird names, as the Tern family has: Swift, Petrel, Albatross.

The Tern itself is drawn from its own data at Star Crew revision 95a49b2, as the yardstick for the family.
