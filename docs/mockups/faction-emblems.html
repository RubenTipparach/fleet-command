Faction Emblems

Each people's mark, as you will meet it: the crest on its console, the small mark beside a contact or a target, and the paint on its hulls. All three are drawn from one set of shapes per faction (docs/mockups/lib/emblems.js), so they always agree. The shapes are first drafts and every meaning is provisional: what a people puts on its flag is your decision, asked in the survey (section E, E1 to E12). OpenSpec change: faction-emblems.

The rules for an emblem

  1. E1
    One mark per people. A faction has one emblem. It names who they are; it never names a job, so it is never used as an icon.
  2. E2
    A field and a device. The outer shape (the field) and one picture inside it (the device), two colours at most, plus holes cut through to the ground.
  3. E3
    It reads in one colour. Every device sits in a window cut 5 units wider than itself on a 100 unit grid, so the mark works as a stencil, as hull paint and at 20 px.
  4. E4
    No two outlines alike. Ring, shield, bitten coin, egg in ribs, drop, stepped throne, triangle, crescent and eye, gauge arch, target brackets: a captain tells them apart by outline alone at 20 px.
  5. E5
    From how the people think. The shape and the colours come from the faction's world view, body and ships (its console skin's design), as its buttons and icons do.
  6. E6
    Three renderings, one source. The crest is the shapes in the skin's material; the mark is the shapes flat; the marking is the shapes in hull paint. Nobody redraws an emblem by hand.
  7. E7
    Sized to be read. At least 20 px wherever it appears on screen, the hull marking included: on a hull that means a height of at least 6 % of the ship's length.
  8. E8
    Our own. No shape is taken from another game, a film or a real nation's or faith's symbol.

Every faction

Each card: the crest at the console's 96 x 96 px slot (a quick stand-in of the skin's material, the skin's own generator renders the real one) beside the crest the console has today; the mark at 40 px and 20 px in colour and in one colour; the hull marking on stand-in plating; and what it means, provisionally.

Side by side, small

All ten at 40 px and 20 px, in colour and in one colour, then the places a small mark sits beside the console's icons: a contacts list and a target's tag. The icons are the Concord skin's, drawn live from docs/mockups/lib/concord.js.

On a ship's flank

A stand-in hull section (not any faction's ship: 60 m of an 88 m frigate's flank, 14 m tall) in each faction's stand-in plating, the emblem painted on both flanks at 6 % of the ship's length, 5.3 m, beside a 1.8 m figure. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. "Battle distance" puts the camera where the whole frigate fills about 40 % of a phone's width, where the mark must still be 20 px.
