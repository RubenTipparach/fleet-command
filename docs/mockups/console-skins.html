Design languages

Concord: what we took from LCARS

Principles, in our words

  • The sweep is the language. An elbow joins a thick bar to a thin one and frames each group of controls. A frame always changes thickness at a turn.
  • A rounded cap ends a bar, and because it ends something it is the button.
  • Break the frame into buttons rather than adding buttons inside it. A frame that only frames is waste.
  • Flat and vector: no bevels, no emboss. Colour glows on black.
  • Five colours at most, each with one meaning; two text colours; three type sizes; two spacing constants.
  • Nothing moves under the hand. A new task is a new screen; fades under 150 ms.

Sources: Wikipedia on LCARS (Michael Okuda; Roddenberry's "not busy" panels), Bracer Jack's LCARS Manifesto and Coherent Interface guideline (lcars-terminal.de), thelcars.com. LCARS is a studio's copyrighted design: we take principles, never its art, typeface or number decor.
