Every freighter is shown twice its drafted size, 300-360 m, its containers 12 by 12 by 24 m; the largest warship, the Krai dreadnought, is 226 m.

Freighters already have work in the game. The campaign map runs each faction's freighters along its trade routes, the convoy missions defend or raid three of them, and a Distress call can come from one. Today they fly as a placeholder.

A spine racks its containers outside along a spine; a cradle parts round them and closes again; the Institute's Twin ledger carries them between two hulls open at the bow.

No faction has a merchant tradition written down, so each concept names a tradition inside its faction, drafted where you have written none, for you to keep, change or reject. None is drawn from a species' body.

Each hull is one grown structure on the same code as the Hunt's and the Institute's ships (Ship Design Bible, chapter 14), in its faction's colours. The containers are the only thing placed, as a crate is set on a deck.

Survey section V asks which concept goes on for each faction, and which traditions stay. Its link is on the tracker.
