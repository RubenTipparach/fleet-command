    How it works

    Version 1 (you: "ensigns provide no bonus but gain xp overtime, and can be traded for prestige so you can get more experienced crew, or just level up your crew member", "need crew skill tree", "skill tree, each level gets a skill point, the player can auto level officers or pick paths on the tree to go down and specialize").

    Ranks run Ensign, Lieutenant, Lt. Commander, Commander, Captain. Each rank above ensign is one skill point, so an ensign brings nothing to a battle. Every officer gains experience from each battle (more for a better result) and a little each turn aboard.

    Promote an officer once they have the experience for the next rank; short of it, the rest can be paid in prestige. Transfer an officer out and the side gains their worth plus their banked experience in prestige, which hires an officer of higher rank from the pool.

    Each station has three branches of three skills. A skill opens once the one above it in its branch is learned, and a Captain has four points, so an officer specialises: one branch to its end and one more step. With Auto level on, points go along the station's own path (the numbers on the tree) as soon as they are earned. Retrain takes every point back for a little prestige.

    After the Starfleet Command 3 manual's officers (printed page 36: officers gain experience over a campaign, their worth and skills rising, and are swapped for more experienced ones for prestige; three skills to a station), in our own words and numbers.
