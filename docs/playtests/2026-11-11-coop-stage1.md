## Determinism across the two machines

- At `13d05cae` the determinism probe (`--checksum`: every scenario 3000 steps) agreed on 38 of 51 scenarios between the
  laptop and the Mac; a networked battle drifted 12 times (`COOP_DESYNC`). The part-by-part probe
  (`--checksum=concord_battle`) put the first difference in the wingmen's positions at step 900: `Math.Sin`, `Cos` and
  `Atan2` round differently in the last bit on Windows x64 and macOS ARM.
- At `5c43b847` every transcendental call in `src/Core` goes through `Sim.DMath` (fdlibm in plain IEEE arithmetic; a test
  refuses any other). The probe's 115 lines and the detail probe's 320 lines are byte-identical on both machines (sha256
  `254c532d...` and `c81a244d...`), and two networked battles ran 283 and 332 comparisons with no desync.
