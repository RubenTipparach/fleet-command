## Found

  recorded on a retry.
- Not covered: phone sizes, overloaded fire.

## Second pass: impacts (2026-11-06)

Owner, of the takes above: "missing explosions when weapon impacts or missing shield reaction when weapon impacts", "the shield
impacts should be pretty visible", "shield explosion wouldn be like a flame, it should be like a shield spark, hull impact would be
a flame explosion".

- **Found**: every particle burst (`Fx.Burst`, `Fx.Streaks`) was created already emitting, so it went off as it was added, at the
  battle's origin, before it was moved to the hit. Every explosion's sparks, smoke and fire, and every shield flare, burst there
  instead: off screen in most battles, on the firing ship in these scenes (it sits at the origin). The flare that did show was a
  pale puff sized to the hull, a few pixels at 20 km, and the shield bands only went from 0.8 to 1 opacity on a hit.
- **Fixed**: bursts start only once placed. A shield hit is a spark: a sharp blue-white pinpoint, blue-white streaks thrown back
  off the shield, blue embers, a short blue light; the struck facing's bands flare white and over-bright. A hull hit is a flame
  explosion: a white-hot fireball burning orange, sparks and smoke. Neither draws smaller than a share of its distance from the
  camera (`Fx.ImpactMinShare`, `Fx.ShieldMinShare`).
- **Takes**: the same eight scenes and script on the same Mac; every run ended `PLAYTEST_RESULT ok`; contact sheet
  `2026-11-06-weapons/contact-impacts.jpg` (six frames of each take, 12 s apart). Seen in the takes: the lance's sparks on the
  Kharz's shield, the torpedo's spark then a fireball when the second one reached the hull, the disruptor's sparks on the
  Resolute and a fireball when a bolt got through, nothing on the firing ship.
