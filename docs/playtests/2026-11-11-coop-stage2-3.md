## What went wrong, and the fix

- **The first campaign take's flagship melted down in most contracts.** The bot never tended the reactor; it now aligns the
  coils, frees a power box (tractor, transporters, sensors) and turns up the coolant. The meltdowns went on, and the cause is
  not the bot: **a frigate's reactor goes long before its hull.** A headless probe of a campaign Patrol: a coolant loop
  destroyed at 50 s with 78 % hull left, the core 115 % at 60 s and 150 % at 70 s (the second loop at a third); twenty
  seconds over 100 % and it melts. Enemy AI ships melt the same way (the Wren, the Ulgar). A balance question for the owner.
- **No pings in the campaign take:** the mission's goals covered the Ping key; it is beside Chat now.
- **The first stage 3 take gave no ship:** the chat box took clicks over its whole rectangle, the crew sheet's ship keys
  under it in a full-size window; it takes the pointer only where it draws now.
- **The crew bot clicked the shakedown's beacons** without the helm (the host refused them, rightly); it no longer does.
- **The first campaign take was restarted:** the admiral's game was stopped by hand, which left OBS recording; OBS was stopped
  through its WebSocket and closed, the partial take deleted.
